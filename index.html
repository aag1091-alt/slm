Update 2026-11-25 — publish bar cleared, v0 model published. The stopping failure was diagnosed to the frozen, tied lm_head/embedding row for the EOS token under QLoRA (the EOS position received no effective gradient); a sub-$1 probe loop exhausted the LoRA-family fixes, and a full-parameter fine-tune (Run 3) fixed stopping as diagnosed: 94.8% clean stops, parse 100%, Batfish 98.8%, intent-match 92.5% triaged on the held-out test split — both legs of the bar (Batfish ≥95%, intent-match ≥90%) cleared. The model is public at huggingface.co/aag1091/netconfig-slm-qwen3-4b with its full evaluation card and a Q4_K_M GGUF build.
